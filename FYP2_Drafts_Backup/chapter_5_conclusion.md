# CHAPTER 5 – Conclusion & Product Roadmap

This chapter concludes the initial release of the system (FYP1) and outlines the project roadmap for the upcoming development phase (FYP2).

## 5.1 Summary of Release 1 (FYP1 Increments)
The completion of Sprints 1 through 6 has successfully delivered a stable, foundational e-commerce platform. The system currently supports:
- Secure authentication and role-based access.
- A fully functional customer shopping experience (Cart & Checkout).
- Efficient administrator tools for bulk inventory management.

These increments meet the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) requirements and are fully shippable.

## 5.2 Novelties of Work
The system differentiates itself from generic e-commerce platforms by its deep integration between the sales interface and the underlying inventory batches. The row-level validation for bulk uploads ensures data integrity, which is a common failure point for small-scale retail systems.

## 5.3 Product Roadmap for FYP2 (Future Sprints)
While Release 1 established the foundation, the upcoming **Release 2** will focus on inventory intelligence. The Product Backlog Items designated for Sprints 7 through 12 include:

- **Sprint 7 & 8 (FEFO Implementation):** Moving from simple stock deduction to expiry-aware batch deduction.
- **Sprint 9 & 10 (Spoilage & Audit):** Implementing real-time logging for damaged and expired goods to maintain 100% stock accuracy.
- **Sprint 11 & 12 (Predictive Analytics):** Developing the restocking algorithm to proactively alert administrators of low stock based on sales momentum.

## 5.4 Conclusion
The Agile Scrum methodology has proven highly effective in managing the transition from conceptual user stories to a working e-commerce system. By focusing on iterative delivery, the project has successfully addressed the foundational business needs of SME grocers, setting a strong technical stage for the advanced inventory controls planned for the next phase.
