# CHAPTER 1 – Introduction (Product Vision & Initiation)

The retail sector’s digital transformation has boosted online grocery shopping due to convenience and accessibility. However, grocery e-commerce platforms struggle with perishable inventory management—challenges like poor stock tracking, absence of expiry logic, and weak predictive restocking cause significant waste. This project focuses on developing an integrated solution tailored for small and medium-sized grocery businesses.

## 1.1 Project Background
The exponential increase in online platforms has transformed food retail, making home delivery a necessity. Store control is critical for maintaining efficiency and customer satisfaction. However, many current systems rely on antiquated tracking and lack automation. This project establishes a single-vendor, web-based grocery e-commerce system to eliminate these challenges through real-time observation and automated inventory logic.

## 1.2 Problem Statement (Business Needs)
The system addresses critical pain points in the current grocery e-commerce landscape:
- **Lack of Expiry-Aware Integration:** Older stock is often sold after newer stock, leading to spoilage.
- **Absence of Spoilage Logging:** Manual logs for damaged goods are often disconnected from live inventory.
- **Reactive Restocking:** Businesses suffer from stockouts or overstocking due to a lack of data-driven forecasting.
- **Manual Overhead:** Updating large inventories item-by-item is inefficient for SME grocers.

## 1.3 Motivation & Contribution (Value Proposition)
This project provides a cost-effective "Product Vision" that empowers small grocers with enterprise-level inventory intelligence. By automating FEFO deductions, spoilage logging, and predictive alerts into a unified platform, the system reduces waste, increases transparency, and builds customer trust without the high cost of proprietary ERP systems.

## 1.4 Product Goals (Objectives)
The final product increment aims to achieve the following goals:
1. **FEFO Deduction:** Prioritize items nearing expiry during checkout.
2. **Spoilage Tracking:** Provide real-time logging for unsellable inventory.
3. **Bulk Integration:** Enable rapid stock updates via validated CSV processing.
4. **Predictive Alerts:** Generate intelligent reorder suggestions based on sales velocity.

## 1.5 Project Scope (MVP Boundaries)
The system is implemented as a single-vendor web application for SME grocery retailers. The core focus (Minimum Viable Product) includes the four features above. Logistics, real payment gateways, and multi-vendor support are excluded from the current product scope to prioritize core inventory accuracy.
