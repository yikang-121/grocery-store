# ABSTRACT

Online grocer websites have seen exponential growth, offering small and mid-sized markets the chance for expanding their businesses; nevertheless, it also brought back old bottlenecks associated with the management of inventory into the limelight, particularly for perishable stocks. Most available solutions do not support expiry-aware stock management, logging of spoilages, predictive reordering, and proper bulk update functionalities. 

To address these gaps, an integrated **E-Commerce and Inventory Management System** was developed specifically for SME grocers. The project was executed in two phases: FYP1 established the core e-commerce foundation (authentication, checkout, bulk upload prototype), while FYP2 delivered advanced inventory intelligence, including **FEFO (First Expired, First Out)** deduction logic, real-time spoilage logging, and a predictive restocking algorithm using adaptive sales velocity metrics.

The system is implemented as a full-stack application using **Spring Boot** (Backend), **Next.js** (Frontend), and **MySQL** (Database). The development followed the **Agile Scrum** methodology across 12 sprints, ensuring iterative testing and progressive refinement of complex batch-level tracking logic. The final evaluation confirms that the system effectively reduces inventory waste, enhances stock transparency, and provides actionable business insights through its forecasting capabilities.

**Area of Study:** Web-Based System Development, Inventory Management Systems
**Keywords:** Grocery E-commerce, FEFO Deduction, Spoilage Logging, Predictive Restocking, Agile Scrum, SME Inventory Solutions.
